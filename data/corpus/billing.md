# Billing and Pricing

## Plans

- **Starter** — $49/month. 1,000 labels included, then $0.10 per additional label.
- **Growth** — $199/month. 5,000 labels included, then $0.08 per additional label.
- **Enterprise** — custom pricing with volume discounts, dedicated support, and an SLA.

Every plan includes unlimited tracking, webhooks, and users. All new accounts start with a 30-day free trial of the Growth plan.

## Invoices

Invoices are issued on the first of each month for the subscription plus the previous month's overage. Pay by card or ACH from Settings > Billing. Failed payments retry for 7 days before the account is paused.

## Canceling

You can cancel anytime from the dashboard. Service continues until the end of the billing period; labels already purchased remain valid.
