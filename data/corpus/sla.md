# Uptime SLA and Support

## Uptime commitment

FleetOps commits to 99.95% monthly API uptime, excluding scheduled maintenance windows (announced 7 days in advance). If uptime falls below 99.95% in a month, affected customers receive a 10% service credit automatically.

## Support tiers

- **Email** — all plans, first response within 24 hours.
- **Live chat** — Growth and Enterprise, business hours, first response within 4 hours.
- **Phone and dedicated Slack** — Enterprise only, with a named support engineer.

## Status page

Real-time status and incident history are published at status.fleetops.io. Subscribe to get notified of incidents by email or webhook.
