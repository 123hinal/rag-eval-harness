# FleetOps Overview

FleetOps is a multi-carrier shipping platform for e-commerce and 3PL businesses. It unifies rate shopping, label printing, shipment tracking, and analytics in a single API and dashboard.

## Key capabilities

- **Rate shopping**: Compare discounted carrier rates across 40+ carriers in one request.
- **Label printing**: Generate PDF, PNG, or ZPL labels, single or in batches of up to 100.
- **Tracking**: Real-time tracking events via API polling or webhooks.
- **Returns**: Branded return portals with QR-code labels and automatic refunds.
- **Analytics**: On-time delivery rates, carrier performance, and cost breakdowns.

## Who it is for

FleetOps serves merchants shipping 500 to 5,000,000 parcels per month. Customers connect their own carrier accounts to unlock negotiated rates, or use FleetOps' pre-negotiated discounts with no account required.

## Regions

FleetOps supports domestic US shipping and international shipping to 220+ countries and territories, including duties and taxes calculation at checkout for 40+ destinations.
